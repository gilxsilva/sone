# Sources and editorial approach

Sone separates research about dreaming, symbolic dictionary associations, and original reflective writing. Dictionary associations are not scientific evidence for a meaning. The reading is assembled with keyword matching and editorial themes, not a clinical assessment or a prediction.

## Research and definitions

- [NHLBI: Stages of sleep](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep/stages-of-sleep): background on REM and non-REM sleep, not symbol meanings.
- [Picard-Deland, Nielsen & Carr (2021): Dreaming of the sleep lab](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34614021/), PLOS ONE, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0257738: dreams can incorporate recent laboratory experiences. The study does not establish a universal symbol code.
- [APA Dictionary: Dream](https://dictionary.apa.org/dream): definition only. It does not validate our symbolic readings.
